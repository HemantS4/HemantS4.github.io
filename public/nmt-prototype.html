Can I hug my kids?

Not yet — give it a few days.

For the first 5 days of your isolation, keep at least 2 metres from young children and pregnant family. Short, calm conversations are fine. On day 6, brief hugs are safe for most people treated with I-131.

Avoid close contact · days 1–5
Brief hugs · day 6+
Normal · day 10
